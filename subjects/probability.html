Probability

Sample spaces, random variables, conditioning, limit theorems — the language every other topic is written in.

3 concepts 12 formulas 12 questions 48%
  1. Conditional Probability The probability of an event once you restrict attention to the states of the world where another event has occurred — the formal machinery for "given what I now know". Foundational ★★★★★
  2. Expectation The probability-weighted average of a random variable — the number a long-run average converges to, and the object almost every pricing and sizing argument is really about. Foundational ★★★★★
  3. Bayes' Theorem The rule for inverting a conditional probability — turning "how likely is this evidence if the hypothesis is true" into "how likely is the hypothesis given the evidence". Foundational ★★★★★
